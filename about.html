How I got here

25+ years of making things run smoothly

I didn't set out to become a virtual assistant, but looking back, every job was pointing me towards it. I started out in administration in 1999, learning the basics in a busy sales office, then spent years in underwriting administration, clerical support, and for a while, leading a warehouse team.

Somewhere in there I noticed a pattern: whatever the job title, I was always the one who ended up organising things, tidying up the systems, and making sure nothing fell through the cracks. So I self-funded an Executive PA diploma to make it official.

After a stint in sales administration and several years as a document controller for engineering firms, I finally set up on my own. These days I get to do the bit I always enjoyed most, minus the bits I didn't, for business owners who actually value it.

Behind the scenes

Outside of client work, I'm a fairly happy nerd. I read a lot, I'm partial to a true crime documentary, and I'll happily go down a research rabbit hole about psychology or art history for no particular reason.

I bring the same instinct to client work: I like understanding how something actually fits together before I try to fix it. It's why systems and quiet organisation are the parts of the job I find most satisfying.
